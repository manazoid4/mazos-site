# Maz Works Design Direction

## Product mode

Persuade through evidence. The site is a working portfolio and client-acquisition surface, not a product dashboard.

## Audience and cadence

Employers, prospective clients, collaborators, and LinkedIn visitors will usually arrive briefly and need to understand Manazir's judgment, shipped work, and next contact route within minutes.

## Visual world

**FRAMEWORK** — warm white architectural planes held by near-black frames. The interface should feel built, measured, calm, editorial, and human.

## Palette family

Paper / charcoal with a restrained signal-yellow accent. Warm ivory is the canvas; charcoal carries structure and grounding; yellow appears only where direction or action matters.

## Type treatment

Sans-first with display-led emphasis: compact uppercase technical labels, large plainspoken headlines, and readable editorial body copy. No decorative pseudo-luxury serif.

## Composition

A framed document: full-width structural bands, repeated project bays, crisp dividers, asymmetric flagship layouts, generous whitespace, and real screenshots treated as evidence.

## Shape language

Crisp geometry, square corners, 1–2px rules, and proportion instead of card decoration. Buttons and proof modules are framed rather than pill-shaped.

## Anti-references

No blue/purple AI gradients, glass panels, glowing blobs, oversized rounded SaaS cards, technology-badge walls, fake terminals, or motion without meaning.

## First-five-seconds contract

Visitors should see: Maz Works, Manazir Hussain, “useful software, AI tools and automation around real problems,” concrete shipped proof, and a clear route to view work or request a bounded free demo.
