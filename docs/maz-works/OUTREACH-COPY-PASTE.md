# NOTE 2 — FAST WORKFLOW + OTHER OUTREACH

## 10-second personalisation rule

Only change the first line.

Use one visible fact:

- “Saw you’ve just opened Christmas bookings 🎄”
- “Saw you’re MCS accredited ☀️”
- “Saw you’ve got 4.4k followers 👀”
- “Saw you’re opening at Nottingham Uni 🎁”
- “Saw your diary fills fast 🐶”
- “Saw you’ve already got online booking ✂️”

Then paste the closest niche message below it.

## Fastest workflow

1. Find business on Instagram, LinkedIn or Google Maps.
2. Spot **one** real detail.
3. Copy closest template.
4. Change first line only.
5. Send.
6. Save the business name + date.
7. Follow up once after 2–4 days.
8. Stop after the second message if there is no reply.

### 8. In-person local outreach

For nearby shops, cafés, barbers, garages, groomers and similar:

Then get their preferred contact method instead of pitching for five minutes.

### 9. Warm comment → DM

Leave a normal, genuine comment first. Do **not** pitch in the comment.

Then DM later using the post as the opener.

Example:

Saw the new [PRODUCT/SERVICE] post 👀 Looks class.

Tiny idea that might save you some admin — want me to send it over?

### 10. Short personalised screen recording

Use only for stronger leads where you can show a real issue in under 60 seconds.

Message:

Spotted one thing that might be costing you enquiries 👀

I recorded a 45-sec look at it — want the link?

Do not send an unsolicited long video. Ask first.

# IMPROVEMENTS TO KEEP USING

- Keep the first message under about 45 words where possible.
- One outcome per message. Do not list every service.
- One joke at most; keep it light and relevant.
- Personalise the **opening**, not the whole pitch.
- Use **Brand Kit** for trainers, creators, bakers, makers, stylists and social-first sellers.
- Use **Maz Works / £195 Starter** for operational businesses with enquiries, bookings, quotes, calls or follow-ups.
- Lead with time saved, enquiries recovered, repeat customers or simpler ordering — not “automation”, “AI” or technical features.
- Free demo is the risk-reversal. Mention it early.
- Avoid fake urgency, fake scarcity or invented ROI.
- Do not send long audits cold. Ask permission first.
- One or two polite follow-ups, then stop.
