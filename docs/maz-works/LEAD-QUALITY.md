# Lead Quality v2 (brief)

Adopted by Maz on 28 Sep 2026. The full canonical rules live in the knowledge vault: `prompts/maz-works-niche-needs.md` (https://github.com/manazoid4/maz-works-knowledge-vault/blob/main/prompts/maz-works-niche-needs.md). This file is a public-safe summary: no prospect names or details ever go in this repo.

**Method:** find a repeated business problem → establish credible evidence → identify a useful paid intervention from Offer v9 (`app/offers.ts`). Cosmetic website issues never qualify a lead on their own.

**Problem families:** enquiries and quoting · bookings and repeat customers · job delivery and invoicing · professional-service projects.

**Score (0–10):** business problem 0–3 · repetition or workload 0–2 · paid solution fit 0–2 · buyer access 0–2 · relevant timing 0–1.

**Gates:** problem ≥ 2, solution fit ≥ 1, verified contact route, evidence from the last 30 days, no exclusion.

**Tiers:** Gold 8–10 · Silver 6–7 · Bronze = re-check queue (not counted). Gold does **not** need a limited company. The contact route is separate: cold email only to a confirmed Ltd/LLP (PECR); otherwise phone or walk-in.

**Never scored:** location, reviews, LinkedIn, gifts, assumed case-study willingness. Unknown budget, urgency and tools stay unknown until the owner says.

Prospect records (evidence, score breakdown, consequence, intervention, unknowns, next action) live only in the private `maz-works-leads` repo and HubSpot.
