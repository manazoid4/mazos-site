## What changed from v9, and why

- **Brand Kit £395 split.** It bundled brand design, a one-page site, payments, Google setup and 12 templates: roughly 25 hours for £395 (about £16 an hour), and it undercut every website price. Brand is now £595 on its own; the page that sells is £895; both together £1,295.
- **Custom Software from £1,950 → £2,950, websites separated.** A portal or staff tool is 40+ hours. Websites got their own offer from £1,950 that includes the enquiry automation, so it is never a "brochure site".
- **Changes: "2 months unlimited" → 30 days of tweaks (two rounds) + 90-day fix promise.** "Unlimited" invited scope creep for 8 weeks after every job. The fix promise keeps the trust; the rounds keep the hours bounded.
- **Add-on floor £95.** Team training £39, Google profile £49, reminders £79 were below the cost of a call + set-up + test + handover. "Confirm or rebook by text" merged into Appointment reminders. Single website page £145 → Extra website page £295 (it is copy + design + build).
- **Extra automation £95 → £145, and three builds quote a Business System.** A second job is real work; three jobs is a system.
- **Care £19/month → £49 and £195/month.** £19 did not cover one hour. Two tiers create recurring revenue and a reason to keep improving.
- **Payment.** Pay when you've seen it working, before go-live; £1,000+ jobs in signed-off stages. Removed "you don't pay the rest" and "refund your deposit" wording (Maz, 1 Oct: splits read as desperate).
- **Referral £40 → 10% of the first project.** Scales with bigger jobs, costs nothing unless a sale happens.
- **Kept on purpose:** Starter Automation at £195 as the way in (fenced to one job, credited toward a Business System). Business System floor £795, now defined as up to three jobs.

## Where Maz was losing money

1. Brand Kit: about 25 hours of brand + web + payments + Google for £395.
2. Two months of unlimited changes on every job, including the £195 Starter.
3. Custom software and websites sharing a £1,950 floor.
4. Add-ons at £39 to £79 that cost more than that to deliver.
5. £19/month care promising a change every month.
6. No staged billing on bigger jobs, while a free demo comes first.

## Do not undo without Maz saying so

- Do not re-bundle brand work with website or payment work.
- Do not bring back "unlimited" changes or a payment split on the site.
- Do not price an add-on under £95, or sell real building as an add-on.
- Do not advertise AI. Do not add testimonials or results that don't exist.
