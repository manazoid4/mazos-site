# 5. Leads and outreach

Lead data (names, contacts, evidence) lives only in the PRIVATE repo `manazoid4/maz-works-leads` and in HubSpot. Never copy it into this public repo or into any post.

## Finding leads (Lead Quality v2)
- Find a repeated business problem with evidence and a named paid fix, not website cosmetics.
- Tiers: Gold 8 to 10, Silver 6 to 7, then Bench. Gold does not need a limited company. Ltd status only decides whether cold email is allowed.
- Creators and makers are valid leads. Someone good at their craft who sells by DM or a marketplace link, with no own website or branded profile, is a creator lead: usually the Sales Page, or Brand + Sales Page if their look is inconsistent (prices in `app/offers.ts`). Upsell later to DM replies, a welcome email series and Keep It Growing.

## Reaching the owner
1. Find the owner by name first (Companies House officers, LinkedIn company page, About or Team page, reviews). Note name, profile URL and one personal hook in HubSpot.
2. Route order: LinkedIn message to the owner (300 characters or fewer), then email to the owner only if a confirmed Ltd, then a phone call asking for the owner by name after a CTPS/TPS check. For creators the natural first route is an Instagram or TikTok DM.
3. One follow-up after 3 days by the next route, logged as a HubSpot task.
4. Never guess or scrape private emails or mobile numbers.

## Message format
- Short sentences, one idea per line, a blank line between them.
- The ask goes on its own line at the end.
- LinkedIn or DM: 2 to 4 short lines. Email: 4 to 6 short blocks.
- In posts: no dashes, link in the first comment.

## Email
Maz sends all outreach from Gmail as info@mazworks.uk. Never send outreach through Resend or another tool. Resend is only for the site's opt-in mail.

## HubSpot standard
- Company names start with the tier: `🥇 GOLD · `, `🥈 SILVER · `, `BENCH · `.
- Every lead is a Task assigned to Maz (type Call, due time, number and a one-line script) with the business website and the exact evidence URL.
- Every lead gets a `📞 PITCH + SCRIPT` note.
- Keep `Lead tier`, `Contact route` and `Website problem` in sync.
