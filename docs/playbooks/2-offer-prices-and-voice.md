# 2. Offer, prices and voice

## Single source of truth
All prices, packages, add-ons, promises and demo steps live in `app/offers.ts`. Change them there only. Never hard-code a price elsewhere. Pages read from it and tests guard it.

## Offer today (Offer v10, 2 Oct 2026)
Read `docs/maz-works/OFFER-V10.md` before changing any price: it records why each number is what it is.

Four kinds of work, never blurred (`TRACKS`):
- **Brand** = how the business looks, sounds and presents itself. Brand & Content Kit £595.
- **Sales pages and websites** = where attention becomes enquiries, bookings or sales. Sales Page £895, Brand + Sales Page £1,295 (bundle), Website from £1,950.
- **Automation** = removes repeat work and joins up the customer journey. Starter Automation £195 (one job, fenced), Business System from £795 (up to 3 joined jobs).
- **Custom software** = what normal tools can't do. Custom Software from £2,950.

- Add-ons £95 to £295, each a set-up on tools the client already has. Floor £95. Extra automation (£145) only adds a job to a package.
- Care: Keep It Running £49/month, Keep It Growing £195/month. Monthly, cancel any time.
- Changes: 30 days of tweaks (two rounds) plus a 90-day fix promise. Never "unlimited".
- Payment: pay when you've seen it working, before go-live; £1,000+ jobs billed in signed-off stages. Never advertise a split like "half now, half later".
- Upgrade credits: Starter price comes off a Business System within 60 days; kit buyers pay only the bundle price for a Sales Page within 60 days.
- Referral: 10% of the referred client's first project.
- Free first step: Free Plan & Fixed Quote (`/leak-check`). Free demo is call-first and dated.

## Positioning (non-negotiable)
- Maz Works builds the systems small businesses run on. It is not a website-fix shop. Never write "repairs", "small fixes", "quick fixes" or "fix your broken link".
- Lead with outcomes: more enquiries become customers, less chasing, hours back.
- The audience is every UK small business, team and creator. Never narrow to one niche. Trades and creators are examples, never a limit. Always offer a "something else" route.
- No AI in the offer. AI may be used behind the scenes, never advertised or sold. Sell the outcome.
- Do not advertise guarantees or payment splits such as "half now, half later" (removed 1 Oct: it reads as desperate). Keep promises to three short lines: free demo first, one fixed price, no contracts (care plans are monthly, cancel any time).
- Never publish location, personal email or phone. Public contact: info@mazworks.uk.
- Prefer free tiers and say plainly when something costs money.

## Voice
- Plain words a busy owner says out loud. Short sentences, one idea each.
- Use the words they know (DM, bookings, reviews, followers), not jargon.
- Be specific to the audience: trainers talk about clients and plans, bakers about orders, salons about no-shows.
- Lead with the lost customer or lost time, then the fix.
- No testimonials or results unless real and sourced.
