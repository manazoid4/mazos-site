# 7. LinkedIn engine

Goal: every weekday, 5 owner leads with a connection note; 3 posts a week. Maz sends and posts by hand.

## Part A: find owners (daily, 5 leads)
```
Using docs/playbooks/5-leads-and-outreach.md (Lead Quality v2) and this playbook, find 5 UK small-business owners on LinkedIn whose business shows a repeated problem I fix: missed calls, slow replies to enquiries, no online booking, quotes not followed up, few reviews, admin copied between apps. Use public pages only (company website, Google reviews, job ads, LinkedIn public profile). Do not log in, connect or message.

For each:
- owner name, LinkedIn URL, business, website
- the problem, with the exact evidence URL
- tier (Gold 8–10 / Silver 6–7 / Bench) and why
- one personal hook from their own words (About line, a post)
- the connection note (template below, 300 characters max)
- the matching fix and price from app/offers.ts

Save to the private repo manazoid4/maz-works-leads under linkedin/YYYY-MM-DD.md and list them as HubSpot tasks to create. Never write lead data into the public site repo.
```

## Connection note (300 characters max)
```
Hi {first name}, saw {hook from their own words}. I build simple systems for small businesses so enquiries don't slip away, like {their problem in 5 words}. Would be good to connect.
```

## After they accept (one message, 2–4 short lines)
```
Thanks for connecting, {name}.

I noticed {specific problem, e.g. the Book now button on your site opens an old page}.

I can show you a free working demo of the fix. Worth a 15-minute call?
```
One follow-up after 3 days by the next route (see playbook 5). Then stop.

## Part B: posts (Mon, Wed, Fri)
```
Write one LinkedIn post for Maz Works following POSTING-RULES.md in manazoid4/unified-memory-database (spine/projects/mazworks-site). Topic: {pick from the list below}. Rules: plain words, short lines, no dashes, no hashtags wall (max 3), no AI claims, no invented results. Put the link (https://www.mazworks.uk/linkedin) in a first-comment line, not the post. Give the post in a plain code block, then the first comment in a second code block.
```
Topic rotation:
1. A real problem seen this week (no business named) and the simple fix
2. A before/after of a free demo (only if real)
3. A "how it works" in 4 steps (call, demo, plan, build)
4. For creators: why "DM to order" loses sales, and the Sales Page fix
5. A lesson from building (JobFilter, Scrap Finance Partners)

## Quality bar
- Every claim true and checkable. No named leads in posts.
- Maz reviews every note and post before it goes out.
