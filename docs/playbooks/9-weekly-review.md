# 9. Weekly review (Monday, 20 minutes)

Goal: keep the week focused on getting paying clients.

## Prompt to paste
```
Weekly review for Maz Works. Gather:
- Enquiries this week (form submissions in Maz's Gmail from FormSubmit/Resend, and `?src=` tags showing where they came from)
- Leads found and messages sent (private repo manazoid4/maz-works-leads, HubSpot tasks)
- Replies, calls booked, demos promised, deals won
- PRs merged on manazoid4/mazos-site
- Vercel analytics: top pages and where visitors dropped off

Then give, in plain words, max 15 lines:
1. Numbers: leads found, messages sent, replies, calls, demos, clients
2. What worked (keep doing)
3. What didn't (stop doing)
4. This week's 3 goals, each with a number (e.g. 50 creator DMs, 2 calls booked)
5. Maz's open to-dos
Save it to the private memory repo under spine/projects/mazworks-site/weekly/YYYY-MM-DD.md.
```

## Targets to start with (adjust after 2 weeks of real numbers)
- 50 creator DMs and 25 LinkedIn notes a week
- 3 calls booked
- 1 free demo delivered
- 1 paying client a month
