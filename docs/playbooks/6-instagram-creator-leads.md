# 6. Instagram creator leads (Brand Kit)

Goal: 10 good creator leads a day, each with a ready DM Maz can send in under a minute.

## Who counts
People who are good at their craft and sell through social media, but look unpolished or lose sales:
- Personal trainers and coaches selling plans (link to Payhip, Stan, Linktree, Google Form)
- Makers and Etsy sellers ("DM to order", Etsy link only)
- Bakers, cake makers, home cooks taking orders by DM
- Hair, nails, lash and brow artists booking by DM
- Photographers, artists, DJs with no own website

## Signals (score 1 point each, 4+ = lead)
1. "DM to order" / "DM for prices" / "link in bio" pointing to someone else's shop
2. No own website (only Linktree, Etsy, Payhip, a Google Form)
3. Inconsistent look (logo, colours, post styles all different)
4. Active: posted in the last 14 days
5. 300–20,000 followers (big enough to earn, small enough to need help)
6. Sells something today (plan, product, booking), not just a hobby
7. UK based (preferred, not required)

## Prompt to paste
```
Find 10 Instagram accounts that match docs/playbooks/6-instagram-creator-leads.md "Who counts" and score 4+ on its signals. Use public search only (Google "site:instagram.com" plus terms like "personal trainer UK DM", "cakes to order DM", "handmade soap Etsy"). Do not log in, follow, like or message anyone.

For each lead give:
- handle and profile URL
- what they sell and where the link goes
- score and which signals
- one specific, kind hook from their own content (a post, their bio, a product)
- the DM, using the template in this playbook, max 4 short lines

Save the list to the private repo manazoid4/maz-works-leads under creators/YYYY-MM-DD.md. Never put it in the public site repo.
```

## DM template (Maz sends, by hand)
```
Hi {first name}, love {specific thing: your 12-week plan / the lavender bar / your nail sets}.

Quick idea: your own simple site with a buy button, so people can pay without DMing you first.

I build these for trainers and makers. Want me to mock one up for you, free?
```
Rules: one DM, no links in the first message, no pitch deck, no price unless asked (Brand Kit £395). If they say yes: book the 15-minute call, agree what the mock-up shows and the date.

## Follow-up (after 3 days, once)
```
Hi {name}, just bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to send the free mock-up if useful. No worries if not.
```

## Quality bar
- Every hook is specific to them. Generic "love your content" = rewrite.
- No invented numbers about them. Only what is visible on their profile.
- Stop at 10. Quality over volume.
