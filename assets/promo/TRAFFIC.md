# Getting more eyes on the posts and the site

Free first; anything that costs money is marked. No bought followers, no engagement pods, no automation that posts or likes for you (LinkedIn bans accounts for it).

## 1. Hashtags (3 to 5 per post, at the very end)
LinkedIn shows posts to people who follow a tag, so mix one broad, one local and one or two niche tags.

| Post | Hashtags |
|---|---|
| 1. Free demo offer | `#SmallBusiness #UKSmallBusiness #Automation #SmallBusinessTips` |
| 2. Missed calls (trades) | `#Tradesmen #Plumbing #Electrician #UKTrades #SmallBusiness` |
| 3. No-shows (salons, clinics) | `#SalonOwner #HairSalon #BeautyBusiness #ClinicOwner #SmallBusiness` |
| 4. How it works | `#SmallBusiness #BusinessOwner #Automation #UKBusiness` |
| 5. What you pay | `#SmallBusiness #UKSmallBusiness #FixedPrice #BusinessOwner` |

Already posted? Tap ··· on the post → Edit → add the tags at the end. Editing doesn't reset the post.

## 2. The first hour decides reach
- Post Tuesday to Thursday, 8 to 9am or 12 to 1pm.
- Link only in the first comment (already doing this).
- Reply to every comment within the hour with a question back, so the thread grows.
- Before you post, leave 5 real comments (two lines or more) on posts by trades or salon owners you'd like as clients. Your name shows up in their feeds.

## 3. Post from your personal profile, then reshare to the page
Personal profiles reach far more people than company pages. Post as Manazir, then reshare to the Maz Works page with one line on top.

## 4. Turn each post into 3 more touches (free)
- **Facebook groups:** local business groups (e.g. "<your city> small business", trades and salon owner groups). Read each group's rules; most allow promo on one set day. Post the picture + 2 lines + "comment DEMO and I'll send you one".
- **Instagram:** the same pictures as a carousel; tags `#smallbusinessuk #tradesman #salonowner`.
- **WhatsApp status / Instagram story:** the free-demo picture with the link sticker (story links are tappable).

## 5. Google (people who are already searching)
- **Submit the site to Google Search Console** (free, about 5 minutes): add `mazworks.uk`, verify via DNS, submit `https://www.mazworks.uk/sitemap.xml`. The 8 new job pages (/services/…) then get found faster.
- **Google Business Profile** (free): a service-area business with no public address (keeps your location private). Category "Business management consultant" or "Software company"; add the services and link to /services.
- **Free directories:** Bing Places (imports from Google), Yell, Bark (paid to reply to leads, marked as cost), Checkatrade is trades-only (not you).

## 6. Direct, highest return
- DM 5 owners a day who fit (trades, salons, clinics) with the free demo, not a pitch: "Want a free demo with <their business> on it? Takes me a day." Follow the owner-first route order in AGENTS.md.
- Every reply to a comment can end with: "Want one with your business name on it?"

## 7. What to track (weekly, 5 minutes)
- LinkedIn: impressions and comments per post (open the post → View analytics).
- Site: enquiries by source (`src=li-…` appears in every enquiry email).
- Keep what works, drop what doesn't, after 2 weeks.
